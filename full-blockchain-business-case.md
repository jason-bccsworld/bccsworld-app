## **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

Full blockchain storage (Option 2) eliminates the need for training centers to maintain physical and digital document storage systems while providing comprehensive AI-powered compliance automation. The business case combines infrastructure cost savings with operational efficiency gains to deliver compelling ROI.

- **Security Systems**: $8K annually (locks, access control, fireproof storage)
- **Insurance**: $5K annually (document protection coverage)
- **Maintenance**: $3K annually (organization, filing, retrieval)
- **Total Physical Storage**: $56K annually

### **Digital Document Storage (Eliminated)**
**Current IT Infrastructure Requirements**:
- **Server Storage**: $20K annually (dedicated storage servers)
- **Backup Systems**: $12K annually (offsite backup services)
- **Security Software**: $8K annually (encryption, access control)
- **IT Support**: $15K annually (system maintenance, updates)
- **Compliance Software**: $10K annually (document management systems)
- **Total Digital Storage**: $65K annually

### **Total Document Storage Elimination**
**Annual Savings**: $121K from eliminated storage infrastructure

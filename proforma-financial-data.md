# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

## Financial Overview
The aviation-first domination strategy projects $250M ARR by Year 5 with $2.5B enterprise valuation, delivering 42x investor returns on $15M Series A investment. The focused approach reduces execution risk while establishing market leadership in the $19.6B global aviation training compliance market.

## Key Financial Metrics
- **Revenue Growth**: 3,333% over 5 years ($3M to $250M ARR)
- **Gross Margin**: 85% by Year 5 (software platform economics)
- **Customer Acquisition**: 1,200 training centers by Year 5 (60% market penetration)
- **Customer Lifetime Value**: $1.2M average over 5 years
- **Break-Even**: Year 3 with 20% net margin

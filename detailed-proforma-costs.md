```
Phase | Period      | New Hires | Monthly Cost | Cumulative Cost
------|-------------|-----------|--------------|----------------
1     | Months 1-6  | 36        | $511,667     | $3,070,000
```

**Key Excel Formulas:**
- Monthly Cost = Annual Salary ÷ 12
- Phase Cost = Monthly Cost × 6 months
- Benefits = Base Cost × 25%
- Total Cost = Base Cost + Benefits

This gives you clean, simple tables to model your $15M investment achieving break-even through strategic hiring phases.

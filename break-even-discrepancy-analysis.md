# LESSONS LEARNED

## Why the Original Estimate Was Wrong

### **1. Theoretical vs. Operational**
- Original: Based on comparative efficiency claims
- Reality: Based on actual aviation market dynamics

### **2. Different Break-Even Definitions**
- Original: Monthly operational break-even only
- Reality: Cumulative investment recovery required

### **3. Overly Optimistic Assumptions**
- Original: Assumed instant market penetration
- Reality: Aviation industry requires patient capital

## The Corrected Investment Story

### **Series A ($15M)**: Foundation to Monthly Break-Even
- **Timeline**: 30 months
- **Outcome**: $800K monthly revenue, monthly profitability
- **Status**: -$1.08M cumulative, needs bridge funding

### **Series B ($15M)**: Scale to True Profitability
- **Timeline**: 18 months additional
- **Outcome**: $250M ARR, cumulative profitability
- **Status**: Market leadership achieved

### **Total**: $30M over 4 years to reach $250M ARR
- **Year 1**: Foundation ($0-$1.2M ARR)
- **Year 2**: Growth ($1.2M-$6M ARR)
- **Year 3**: Scale ($6M-$50M ARR)
- **Year 4**: Domination ($50M-$250M ARR)

The discrepancy exists because the original estimate confused **monthly break-even** (Month 16) with **cumulative break-even** (Month 36), while also making overly optimistic assumptions about aviation market penetration speed.

# PATENT DEFENSIVE VALUE

## **Licensing vs. Litigation Protection**

### **Defensive Licensing Benefits:**
- **Litigation Prevention**: Licensed companies less likely to challenge patents
- **Market Validation**: Licensing deals validate patent strength and commercial value
- **Industry Relationships**: Licensing creates strategic partnerships vs. adversarial relationships
- **Revenue Certainty**: Predictable licensing income vs. uncertain litigation outcomes

### **Cross-Licensing Opportunities:**
- Exchange patent rights with complementary technology companies
- Joint development of new patent applications
- Shared patent prosecution costs for related technologies
- Mutual protection against patent trolls and competitors

## Key Insights
- **Engineering** represents the largest investment (31% of total costs)
- **Sales/Customer Success** is the second largest (35% of total costs)
- **Leadership** has highest per-employee cost ($421,875 per person)
- **Monthly burn rate** peaks at $1.15M in Month 18
- **Break-even achieved** when revenue reaches $1M monthly
- **Self-funding begins** Month 19 with positive cash flow

This cost structure ensures efficient capital deployment while building the team needed to reach $12M ARR and break-even by Month 18.

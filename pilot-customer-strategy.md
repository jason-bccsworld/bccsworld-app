# Pilot Customer Testing Strategy: Pre-Investment Validation

## Executive Summary

Before presenting to investors, we need 3-5 Part 142 training centers actively using BCCS142 with measurable results. This pilot program will provide concrete validation metrics, customer testimonials, and revenue proof points that investors require for Series A funding.

## Pilot Program Objectives

### **Primary Goals**
- **Revenue Validation**: Generate $50K-$100K ARR from pilot customers
- **Product-Market Fit**: Achieve 90%+ customer satisfaction and retention
- **Use Case Validation**: Prove regulatory compliance value with authentic FAA audit results
- **Reference Customers**: Secure 3-5 customers willing to speak with investors

### **Success Metrics**
- **Customer Retention**: 100% of pilot customers renew
- **Net Promoter Score**: 70+ (indicating strong product-market fit)
- **Time to Value**: Customers see compliance improvements within 30 days
- **Revenue Growth**: 40%+ monthly growth during pilot phase

## Target Pilot Customers

### **Ideal Pilot Profile**
- **Size**: 50-200 students (manageable scale for testing)
- **Location**: US-based Part 142 training centers
- **Pain Points**: Recent regulatory issues or upcoming FAA audits
- **Technology**: Basic willingness to adopt new software
- **Decision Maker**: Direct access to Chief Operating Officer or Training Manager

### **Specific Target Candidates**

#### **Tier 1 Prospects (High Priority)**
1. **FlightSafety International** (Partner Centers)
   - Regional training centers with compliance challenges
   - Existing relationship through simulator partnerships
   - High credibility for investor references

2. **CAE Training Centers**
   - Independent training centers using CAE simulators
   - Technology-forward approach to training
   - Global footprint for expansion validation

3. **ATP Flight School Locations**
   - Fast-growing Part 141/142 hybrid operation
   - Technology adoption culture
   - Multiple locations for scalability testing

#### **Tier 2 Prospects (Regional Focus)**
4. **Pan Am International Flight Academy**
   - Established reputation with regulatory expertise
   - International student base
   - Complex compliance requirements

5. **Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University** (Part 142 Division)
   - Academic credibility for investor presentations
   - Research partnership potential
   - Student population for AeroStudent module testing

- Establish baseline compliance metrics

**Deliverables**:
- Fully operational BCCS142 deployment

- Baseline compliance assessment completed

**Objective**: Prove measurable compliance improvements

**Activities**:
- Monitor regulatory compliance improvements
- Track time savings from automated processes
- Document audit preparation efficiency gains
- Gather customer feedback and testimonials
- Refine product based on real-world usage

**Deliverables**:
- Quantified compliance improvement metrics
- Customer success stories and testimonials
- Product refinements based on feedback
- Initial revenue recognition

### **Phase 3: Expansion Testing (30 days)**

**Activities**:

- Validate blockchain record integrity
- Prepare comprehensive case studies
- Secure investor reference commitments

**Deliverables**:
- Complete platform ecosystem validation
- Detailed ROI case studies
- Investor-ready customer references
- Proven scalability metrics

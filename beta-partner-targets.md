## CONTACT RESEARCH METHODOLOGY

### LinkedIn Research Strategy

**Search Queries**:
- "Chief Training Officer" + [Company Name]
- "Director Flight Training" + [Company Name]
- "VP Training" + [Company Name]
- "Training Manager" + [Company Name]
- "Compliance Manager" + [Company Name]

**Profile Identification**:
- Current role at target organization
- Aviation industry experience
- Training/compliance responsibilities
- Recent activity and engagement
- Connection network analysis

### Email Research Tools

**Email Finder Tools**:
- Hunter.io for domain-based email discovery
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator for direct messaging
- Company website contact directories
- Industry association member directories
- Conference speaker listings

**Email Verification**:
- NeverBounce for email validation
- ZeroBounce for deliverability checking
- Manual verification through LinkedIn messaging
- Phone verification for high-value targets

### Phone Research Strategy

**Number Sources**:
- Company website contact information
- LinkedIn profile contact details
- Industry directory listings
- Conference registration databases
- Professional association directories

**Calling Strategy**:
- Morning calls (9-11 AM local time)
- Avoid Monday mornings and Friday afternoons
- Prepare voicemail scripts for callbacks
- Track call outcomes and follow-up timing
- Use local numbers when possible

## BETA PARTNER SELECTION CRITERIA

### Must-Have Criteria
- **Current aviation certification** (FAA/EASA/Transport Canada)
- **Active training operations** (50+ students annually)
- **Digital infrastructure** (basic computer systems)
- **Leadership commitment** (C-level or director involvement)
- **Feedback capability** (willing to provide detailed input)

### Preferred Criteria
- **Multi-location operations** (compliance coordination benefits)
- **Technology adoption** (existing digital systems)
- **Industry influence** (reference customer value)
- **Growth trajectory** (expanding operations)
- **International presence** (global market validation)

### Ideal Criteria
- **Innovation leadership** (early technology adoption)

- **Partnership history** (collaborative approach)

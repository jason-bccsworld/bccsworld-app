# EXCEL FORMULAS FOR PROFORMA

## Monthly Calculations
- **Monthly Salary Cost** = Annual Salary ÷ 12
- **Phase Cost** = Monthly Cost × Phase Duration
- **Benefits** = Phase Cost × 25%
- **Total Cost** = Phase Cost + Benefits

## Cash Flow Calculations
- **Monthly Burn** = Sum of all monthly costs in phase
- **Cumulative Burn** = Running total of monthly burn
- **Net Cash Flow** = Monthly Revenue - Monthly Burn
- **Cumulative Cash** = Running total of net cash flow

## Break-Even Calculations
- **Monthly Break-Even** = Monthly Revenue ≥ Monthly Burn
- **Cumulative Break-Even** = Cumulative Revenue ≥ Cumulative Investment

# BCCS Regulator - Separate Regulatory Oversight Application
## Real-Time Compliance Monitoring and Analytics Platform

### Executive Summary

**Separate Application Justification:**
- **Data Scale**: Real-time monitoring of hundreds of Part 142 organizations requires dedicated infrastructure
- **Security Requirements**: Regulatory oversight needs isolated, auditable access controls
- **Processing Power**: Advanced analytics, AI trend analysis, and machine learning require specialized compute resources
- **Regulatory Independence**: Must operate independently from training organizations to ensure objectivity

## Platform Architecture

### Core Modules

#### 1. Predictive Analytics Engine
- **AI-Powered Failure Prediction**: Machine learning models analyzing sensor data, maintenance history, and operational patterns
- **Cross-Fleet Intelligence**: Learning from one aircraft improves predictions for entire network
- **Real-Time Risk Assessment**: Continuous monitoring with dynamic priority adjustment
- **Digital Twin Integration**: Virtual aircraft models for scenario testing

#### 2. Regulatory Compliance Integration
- **Maintenance Documentation**: Automatic generation of regulatory-compliant maintenance records
- **Inspection Scheduling**: AI-optimized compliance with FAA/EASA requirements
- **Audit Trail Blockchain**: Immutable maintenance history for regulatory verification
- **BCCS142 Integration**: Seamless connection with existing compliance platform

#### 3. Supply Chain Optimization
- **Smart Inventory Management**: Predictive parts ordering based on failure forecasts
- **Supplier Risk Intelligence**: Early warning system for supply chain disruptions
- **Parts Authentication**: Blockchain verification to eliminate counterfeit components
- **Cross-Operator Parts Sharing**: Network marketplace for urgent parts needs

#### 4. Workforce Intelligence
- **Technician Skill Matching**: AI assignment of maintenance tasks based on expertise and certification
- **Training Needs Prediction**: Proactive identification of skill gaps
- **Workload Optimization**: Balanced scheduling across maintenance teams
- **Regulatory Currency Tracking**: Automatic monitoring of technician certifications

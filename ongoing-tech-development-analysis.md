For a functioning enterprise SaaS like BCCS142, ongoing development costs are **$9,000-12,000/month** versus **$28,000+/month** during initial build. This 65-70% reduction reflects the natural evolution from intensive development to steady maintenance and enhancement.

The platform becomes increasingly efficient as it matures, with customer-funded premium features often generating more revenue than the base development costs, creating a profitable development operation.

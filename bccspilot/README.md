# BCCSPilot - AI-Powered Pilot Workforce Planning

BCCSPilot is a standalone platform that revolutionizes airline pilot workforce planning through AI-driven predictive analytics, retirement forecasting, and market intelligence.

## 🎯 Key Features

### **Predictive Analytics**
- **Retirement Forecasting**: AI-powered individual pilot retirement risk scoring
- **Market Demand Prediction**: Route growth and fleet expansion analysis
- **Training Timeline Optimization**: Perfect timing for hiring vs. operational needs
- **Cost Scenario Modeling**: Financial impact analysis of different staffing strategies

### **Market Intelligence**
- **Real-time Salary Trends**: Current compensation by position and aircraft type
- **Competitor Activity Tracking**: Industry hiring activities and strategies
- **Training Capacity Monitoring**: Pipeline analysis across major providers
- **Regulatory Change Impact**: Policy updates affecting pilot requirements

### **Decision Support**
- **Hiring Optimization**: Data-driven recommendations for pilot recruitment
- **Training Schedule Planning**: Batch optimization for cost efficiency
- **Risk Mitigation**: Shortage/surplus scenario planning
- **ROI Analysis**: Training investment optimization

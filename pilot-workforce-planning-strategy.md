# BCCSMaint Pilot Workforce Planning: AI-Powered Hiring Optimization

## 🎯 Market Opportunity Overview

The pilot shortage crisis presents a **massive opportunity** for BCCSMaint to expand beyond maintenance into workforce planning - a natural extension of our predictive capabilities that addresses one of aviation's most critical challenges.

### **Current Industry Crisis (2025)**
- **28,126 cumulative pilot shortage by 2030**
- **16,000+ retirements over next 5 years** (mandatory age 65)
- **$150K-200K training cost per pilot**
- **2-3 years timeline** from start to airline-ready
- **Nearly 2,000 mandatory retirements** at legacy airlines in 2025 alone

### **Financial Impact**
- **$200 billion industry investment** in training/professional services through 2042
- **Average pilot compensation**: $352K-388K annually at major airlines
- **Training ROI crisis**: Airlines investing massive capital with uncertain timing
